# Literature notes

## Music and physiology

- Karageorghis & Priest (2012) - music can reduce perceived exertion during exercise
- Terry et al. (2020) - meta-analysis, music improves performance ~7%
- Ballman (2021) - high-tempo music increases workout intensity

## HRV and recovery

- Buchheit (2014) - HRV as recovery marker, validated against subjective scales
- Stanley et al. (2013) - sleep HRV correlates with next-day readiness

## Gap

Post-workout (not during workout) music effects on physiological recovery are
under-studied. Most music research focuses on the workout window itself.
